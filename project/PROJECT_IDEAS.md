# Project Ideas

These 12 ideas. Each describes the intended users, core web application, and proposed AI capabilities.
The source's labels are retained, including its unnumbered planner, option 3.5, and
the jump from option 9 to option 11.

These are source-document concepts, not changes to the course requirements. The
[current project guidelines](README.md) exclude AI features and several integrations
described here; use those guidelines when defining a course project’s scope.

## Student Academic and Advising Planner

Help students understand degree requirements, plan courses across semesters, and
make informed decisions with their advisors. Students build and revise plans with
prerequisite checks, time-slot filtering, and scenarios such as adding a minor.
Advisors use a shared dashboard to review progress, milestones, and advising notes,
with permissions controlling access.

The proposed AI advisor generates valid four-year plans, explains workload and
course bottlenecks, and answers questions about prerequisites, major changes, and
study abroad. It also prepares advising briefs covering student status, risks, and
next steps. Responses cite retrieved curriculum rules and program documents.

## 1. Academic and Career Pathway Coaching

Help students connect their academic choices to internships, careers, and graduate
school. Students maintain profiles with interests, skills, resumes, coursework, and
academic status. They browse opportunities and career resources, track applications
and deadlines, and manage advising appointments. Academic and career advisors can
review profiles, plans, and engagement history.

The proposed AI coach recommends career paths and graduate programs, explains how
they fit a student’s background, and compares options. It provides resume and
LinkedIn feedback, drafts outreach messages, and suggests skills and courses to
pursue. Recommendations cite job postings, alumni outcomes, and institutional
career resources.

## 2. Club, Activity, and Study Group Recommender

Help students discover extracurricular activities and study groups that fit their
interests, schedules, and goals. The web application lets students browse and
filter groups by interest area, time commitment, and meeting schedule, save
favorites, and view upcoming events.

The proposed AI agent asks students about their interests and availability, then
recommends suitable groups with explanations. It suggests concrete next steps,
such as attending an upcoming meeting or drafting an outreach message.
Recommendations draw on club descriptions, schedules, and historical engagement
data to support meaningful participation outside the classroom.

## 3. Integrated Syllabus, Assignment, and Announcement Consolidator

Give students one place to find course deadlines and announcements. Students
register sources such as learning management systems, course websites, and other
course tools. A unified dashboard displays assignments, exams, and announcements,
with links back to the original information.

The proposed AI agent reads syllabi and announcements, extracts deadlines into a
consistent format, and summarizes updates. A weekly overview highlights the most
relevant work and changes so students can stay organized. Every summary cites the
original syllabus section or announcement, allowing students to verify details.

## 3.5. Credential Recognition for Transfer Credit and Prior Learning

Help transfer and current students receive credit for demonstrated competencies
from coursework, certifications, military training, and experiential learning.
Students submit records and portfolios, track requests and awarded credits, and
see remaining requirements. Evaluators review equivalencies and approve, deny,
or partially award credit. The application supports both incoming transfer
cohorts and individual course petitions. The source identifies this as a
high-priority core idea.

The proposed AI agent extracts learning outcomes, maps competencies to the ESCO
skills framework, and compares them with institutional course and degree
requirements. It recommends transfer or prior-learning credit and ways to close
competency gaps, with advisor-reviewable explanations and citations to records,
framework definitions, and policies. Bias detection and equity analysis flag
differences in recognition rates across student populations.

## 4. Intelligent Student Task and Time Management

Help students balance academic and personal responsibilities through a task
management application. Students create, categorize, prioritize, and track tasks,
with deadlines and reminders supporting day-to-day organization.

The proposed AI agent turns those tasks into a realistic schedule based on
priorities and deadlines. It learns from past task-completion patterns and adjusts
plans when work is delayed or new tasks arrive. Students can review explanations
for scheduling decisions and override recommendations, keeping control over
their time while managing workload and avoiding burnout.

## 5. Group Formation, Scheduling, and Collaboration Support

Help students form teams and coordinate group work fairly and efficiently. The
web application manages membership, availability, meetings, and task assignments,
giving team members a shared view of responsibilities and schedules.

The proposed AI agent suggests balanced teams and workload distributions based on
skills and interests. It produces meeting summaries and action items, then
proposes follow-up meetings when tasks remain unresolved. Recommendations include
transparent explanations and require human confirmation, so students retain
control over team composition and collaboration decisions.

## 6. Curriculum Benchmarking and Program Comparison

Help program administrators compare their curriculum with peer institutions to
identify strengths, gaps, and opportunities. The web application presents
structured comparisons of course offerings, degree requirements, and program
focus areas across institutions.

The proposed AI agent analyzes peer catalogs and answers questions about where a
program is ahead or behind. It generates curriculum gap analyses to support
program review and improvement. All findings cite catalog descriptions and
official program documents so administrators can inspect the evidence behind
each comparison.

## 7. Accreditation Feedback and Continuous Improvement Tracking

Help accreditation coordinators document feedback and follow improvement work
through to completion. Users record accreditation findings, action items,
responsible parties, and supporting evidence, while tracking status and deadlines.
This creates a record of how the institution responds to findings over time.

The proposed AI agent maps uploaded evidence to accreditation standards and
identifies missing or weak support. It also drafts narrative sections for
self-study reports, citing the relevant documents. Coordinators can use those
drafts and evidence checks to prepare a clearer account of continuous improvement.

## 8. Course and Instruction Benchmarking with Student Insights

Help faculty improve course quality using student performance and feedback. The
web application provides dashboards showing grades, enrollment trends, and
structured student feedback, allowing instructors to review evidence about
their courses.

The proposed AI agent combines anonymized student feedback with course materials
to identify recurring themes and opportunities for improvement. It offers
evidence-based suggestions supported by representative citations. The goal is
to help instructors turn feedback and course data into concrete teaching
improvements.

## 9. Research, Career, and Opportunity Matching Assistant

Help graduate students and faculty discover research, career, and funding
opportunities aligned with their backgrounds and goals. Users browse and track
grants, internships, jobs, and institutional opportunities in one application.

The proposed AI agent matches resumes or research profiles to opportunity
descriptions and explains why each match is relevant. It drafts materials for
the next step, including cover letters, concept memos, and outreach emails.
Matches and generated materials are grounded in retrieved opportunity
descriptions with citations, making the basis for each recommendation visible.

## 11. Faculty Annual Review and Activity Reporting Assistant

Help faculty prepare annual reviews that accurately represent teaching, research,
and service with less administrative work. Faculty enter or import publications,
grants, teaching assignments, evaluations, and service roles, and upload supporting
documents. Chairs and administrators review submissions, provide feedback, and
track completion.

The proposed AI agent uses CVs, previous reports, teaching records, and institutional
data to draft narratives aligned with review criteria. It highlights accomplishments
and changes since the previous review, flags poorly documented areas, and answers
questions about research trends or alignment with promotion and merit criteria.
Summaries cite faculty materials and institutional guidelines.
